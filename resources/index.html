Cratering Notebook

A Jupyter notebook for simulating a population of impact craters on a given planetary surface, where the topography is a LandLab model grid object. Users go through a series of steps to construct realistic landscapes that also include hydrological evolution. Built by me and Emily Bamber (UT Austin) during the ESPIn Summer School.
